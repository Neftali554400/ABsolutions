Here's the full design asset for the AB Solutions refresh. Here's a breakdown of the decisions:

Typography — DM Serif Display + DM Sans The original site uses generic, dated serif/sans combos. DM Serif Display brings editorial authority and warmth — perfect for a consultancy that wants to feel experienced and trustworthy, not corporate-cold. DM Sans (with optical sizing 9–40) handles all UI text: clean, professional, and highly legible at small sizes.

Color palette Your existing Navy + Red brand identity is strong — it was kept and refined. The additions:

  • A mid-navy ramp for depth in navbars and icon backgrounds
  • A light red tint (#F5E6E4) for badges, tags, and hover states
  • Slate gray for body text (less harsh than pure black)
  • A subtle dark gold accent (#B8860B) for premium callouts like certifications or featured stats

Icons Replaced the generic symbol-style icons with Tabler Outline icons — each one mapped 1:1 to an existing AB Solutions service. They're sharper, more consistent at all sizes, and work great in nav and service cards.

Design style updates

  • Flat, high-contrast cards with hairline borders instead of heavy shadows
  • Serif display type with a bold red underline accent for section headings
  • Stat cards using the serif display font to signal authority
  • A dark navy nav bar to anchor the page instead of the current light header
  • Spacing scale follows a strict 4px base unit for consistent rhythm across all sections

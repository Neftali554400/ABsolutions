What We Do

Comprehensive Agency
Operations Services

From Epic system setup to ongoing staff development, we cover every facet of your agency's operations — so you can focus on serving your clients.

Agency Management System Setup & Communication

Your Agency Management System is a powerful and flexible tool. But it must be set up properly in order to realize its full potential. We leverage decades of experience across hundreds of agencies to provide perspective on optimal system configuration.

Reporting & Data Extraction

Building reports and data extraction needs can be difficult and time-consuming. We assist with internal Epic reports and Bulk Data Extract functions.

Proposals & Form Letters

Integrated Proposals and Form Letters will save staff time and reduce your agency's E&O exposure. Hand-crafted documents consume significant staff resources annually.

Accounting Services

For any business to succeed over the long term, accurate financial records are not optional. We provide staff training, problem area cleanup, Company Payables, Accounts Receivables, and Producer Payables management.

Staff Training

We provide instruction on procedures and the advanced agency-specific processes we've helped develop for your agency.

Outsourced Accounting

If the time and expense required to have well-trained in-house accounting staff are too high, we can take over the routine handling of the more difficult accounting tasks for your agency.

Agency Management Data Clean Up

A neat and orderly agency management system is an essential component for maximizing staff productivity. We help remove outdated items from key areas including Activities, Attachment Folders, Policy Types, Statuses, Templates, and Companies.

Quality Assurance Programs

A formal quality assurance program must be implemented to maximize the return on your investment in procedures and staff training. We build structured QA programs based on your written procedure manuals.

Procedures Development

We lead collaborative on-site meetings with management and staff to create customized procedure manuals through group discussion — not generic templates.
